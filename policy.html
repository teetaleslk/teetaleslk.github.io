Delivery & Policy

Everything you need to know before you order.

🚚 Islandwide Delivery 🏦 Bank Transfer Payment 🔄 Easy Exchanges 💬 WhatsApp Support

🚚 Delivery

We deliver islandwide across Sri Lanka via registered courier post.
· Colombo & suburbs: usually 2–4 business days after order confirmation
· Outstation areas: usually 4–7 business days
Delivery times can vary slightly during peak seasons — we'll always keep you posted on WhatsApp.

🏦 Payment

TeeTales orders are confirmed and paid via bank transfer. Once you send your order on WhatsApp, we'll confirm stock and share our account details there — nothing is charged automatically, and no card or payment details are ever collected on this website.

🔄 Exchange Policy

Wrong size? Let us know within 3 days of delivery and we'll arrange a size exchange, as long as the tee is unworn, unwashed, and has its tags attached. Since every tee is made to order, we're unable to offer refunds for a simple change of mind — but we'll always work with you to make it right.

✅ Quality Guarantee

Every tee is single-jersey cotton with a vibrant DTF print. If anything arrives damaged or misprinted, message us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out — free of charge.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the item and size — or tap "Order on WhatsApp" on any product page, or add a few things to your cart first. We'll confirm everything with you before it ships.

How do custom tees work?

Send us your idea, an image, or even your child's drawing on the Custom Order page or on WhatsApp. We'll confirm the design and sizes with you before printing — nothing prints until you're happy with it.

How do I pick the right size?

Every product page has a cm size chart. A quick trick: lay a tee you already own flat and measure armpit-to-armpit — compare that to our chart to find your closest match.

What's the fabric like?

Single Jersey cotton with a vibrant DTF (direct-to-film) print. Soft, breathable, comfortable for everyday wear. For the longest life, wash inside-out on a gentle cycle and avoid ironing directly over the print.

What if the size or design isn't right when it arrives?

Message us within 3 days of delivery and we'll arrange a size exchange, as long as the tee is unworn, unwashed, and still has its tags. If anything arrives damaged or misprinted, send us a photo and we'll sort it out free of charge.

Do you deliver islandwide?

Yes — via registered courier post. Colombo & suburbs usually take 2–4 business days, outstation areas 4–7 business days, after your order is confirmed. We'll keep you posted on WhatsApp throughout.

Ordering for a group, office, or event?

Bulk pricing unlocks automatically once you have 5 or more tees in your cart — mix any sizes or designs. For larger corporate or event orders, our Bulk Order page is the fastest way to get a quote.

💬 Questions?

We reply within 1 hour, 9am–9pm. Reach us any time on WhatsApp.
